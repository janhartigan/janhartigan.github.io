Privacy policy

This application is for personal use by its owner only, to view and control the owner's own Tesla vehicles and energy products.

  • Data from Tesla (vehicle and energy status, location) is used only to give the owner information and to run commands they request.
  • Data is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.
  • Tokens and data are stored on the owner's private server and nowhere else. No analytics or tracking runs on this site.
  • Access can be revoked anytime at tesla.com (Account Security → Third-Party Apps) or by removing the key on the vehicle's Locks screen.

Last updated: October 2026.
